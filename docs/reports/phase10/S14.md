## Harness

`npm run s14:check`: `engine\src\devtools\s14-check.ts` (the steps), `s14-check-cli.ts`, `s14-config.ts` (the plan), `s14-charts.ts`, `s14-curve.ts`, `s14-grading.ts`, `s14-hsl.ts`, `s14-keys.ts`; test `engine\tests\s14-check.test.ts`. It uses existing bridge commands only (plugin 0.17.0 or later), with no plugin change. It reuses S10's census, range probe (`rangeProbeOf`) and select-snapshot-put-back battery (`behindSnapshot`). Jim's steps: `spikes\S14\README.md`.

- **Charts.** sharp writes two 1800 x 1200 8-bit sRGB PNGs: a grey wedge (21 columns, level `round(255 i / 20)`) and a hue chart (360 patches, HSV hue 0-359 at S 0.7, V 0.9, 24 x 15). They are imported in place from `%TEMP%\LrC-AVG\S14\charts\` into the collection "LrC-AVG S14 charts". Each export is measured as the mean colour of the middle half of every cell, at the session's preview size (1600 px, quality 75).
- **a. Curve.** Seven variants on the NEF and on the wedge, each starting from the snapshot. They are listed in `s14-curve.ts` `CURVE_VARIANTS`: #187's curve alone; with `ToneCurveName2012 "Custom"` [unverified guess, #187]; plus `EnableToneCurve`; a 3-point and a 16-point curve; the red channel; `ParametricDarks`/`ParametricLights`. For each variant the check records the read-back (`taken`) and the 21 column levels of the render. `render_changed` means the mean luma change is over 1.5 levels.
- **b. Grading.** Each wheel on the wedge: shadows (`SplitToningShadow*`, columns 3-6), midtones (`ColorGradeMidtone*`, columns 9-11), highlights (`SplitToningHighlight*`, columns 14-17). Hue goes 0, 30 … 330 at saturation 60. The check measures the cast's sRGB hue and strength against the unedited render, then fits `measured = direction x set + offset`.
- **c. HSL.** On the hue chart, each `SaturationAdjustment<band>` is set to -100 in turn. Each patch's loss of HSV saturation gives the band's centre, its FWHM and its extent (loss > 0.1).
- **d. Mixer.** The pinned Monochrome pair is written: Adobe Monochrome on the NEF, Monochrome on the JPEG. If no `GrayMixer*` key appears, `ConvertToGrayscale: true` is written as well (see run 1 below). After each write the check measures the render's mean HSV saturation over 8 x 8 cells; near 0 means black and white. It saves the settings as `census\s14_monochrome_*.json` (S5 dump shape), then probes the `GrayMixer*` keys at ±100 and 1 % beyond.
- **e. Calibration.** `ShadowTint` and `Red/Green/BlueHue/Saturation` are probed at ±100 and 1 % beyond, on the NEF and the JPEG.

The ±100 candidate limits are [unverified]: our reading of Lightroom's sliders, which the run checks.

Results go under `%TEMP%\LrC-AVG\S14\`: `s14_check_<time>.json`, `s14_bridge_log_<time>.txt` and `census\`. Claude Code collects them into `docs\reports\phase10\S14\`.

### b. Grading wheels

- **The `SplitToning*` keys drive the Shadows and Highlights wheels.** Jim saw 120 / 80 in the Shadows wheel after the write [stated: the "y" answer]. Every sweep write was taken and tinted that wheel's tonal range [handle: `photos[2].grading.wheels[*].steps`].
- **A wheel's hue is close to the sRGB hue of the tint it gives**, the same way on all three wheels. The fit was `measured = direction x set + offset`, over 12 hues per wheel [handle: `wheels[*].convention`]:

| Wheel (keys) | direction | offset | rms | Largest misses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shadows (`SplitToningShadow*`) | +1 | -0.2° | 11.6° | 120 → 139, 210 → 191 |
| Midtones (`ColorGradeMidtone*`) | +1 | 0.5° | 12.3° | 120 → 141, 210 → 194 |
| Highlights (`SplitToningHighlight*`) | +1 | 0.4° | 11.8° | 120 → 140, 210 → 196 |

  So the two hue scales agree in direction and in where 0° is, but in between they differ by up to about 20°, near green and between cyan and blue. A tint's hue read from a render therefore maps back to a wheel hue within about ±20° [inference: from the 12 pairs].
- **Each wheel's tonal reach on the wedge** (mean tint strength over the 12 hues, column 0 black to 20 white) [handle: `steps[*].column_strengths`]:
  - shadows: peak at column 5 (level 64), half strength from about columns 2 to 8;
  - midtones: peak at columns 10-11 (levels 128-140), half strength from about 7 to 14;
  - highlights: peak at column 16 (level 204), half strength from about 13 to 19.

  Each wheel reaches into the next one's range.
